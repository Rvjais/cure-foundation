Cancer Care Units Research & Educational Foundation

About

Us

.

CURE Foundation is a spirited initiative to create consciousness on cancer prevention, early detection, cure & rehabilitation especially for the needful. In addition to spreading awareness for the benefit of the society, the Foundation has so far made available, quality cancer treatment, both free and subsidized, to more than 1700 needy patients, and engaged in numerous rehabilitation, research and education programs. CURE is a non-profit organization.

Our Vision

  • To Make Professional Cancer care accessible and equitable for every individual

Our Inspiration

Dr. Vijay Anand Reddy, who is presently the Director and Senior Consultant Oncologist at Apollo Cancer Hospital, Hyderabad, is unquestionably at the academic pinnacle of oncology in India and is an inspirational figure in medical circles. He is an astute clinician and healer with over two decades of experience that have made him a caring and enthusiastic cancer crusader – empowering his patients with love, hope, faith, courage, peace of mind, and the spirit to conquer cancer. The doctor has handled more than 20,000 cases, providing his patients with customized evidence-based and experience-driven protocols that have led to his extraordinary success rate of more than 85%. Dr. Reddy has always been empathetic towards those less fortunate in society. He has founded CURE Foundation to increase awareness of cancer prevention, early detection, and rehabilitation, and to make quality cancer treatment more readily available, especially to the needy.
